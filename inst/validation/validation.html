Validation

How to validate

Records (one per species and day) are sorted from the least plausible to the most plausible: species unusual at this site and week according to BirdNET, or with low confidence, come first. Listen to the clips (the dashed lines of the spectrogram mark the 3 s window of the detection) and decide:

  • Valid (V): the species is right. It will be exported to Observation.org even with a single detection, marked as certain.
  • Not valid (N): it is not that species. The record will not be exported.
  • Unsure (U): the usual criteria of export_observation() apply.
  • Other species: type its name and pick it from the list; the record is exported under that species.

Move with J/K (or ↓/↑), play the first clip with Space. Your decisions are kept in this browser. When you finish (or to continue on another computer), press Download decisions and run export_observation(am) in R: it finds the file in the validation folder or in your Downloads folder.
